Project methodology

Evidence before judgment.

HireLens AI is designed around one principle: AI should organize evidence, not replace human judgment.

No evidence, no claim. When a resume does not provide enough evidence, the system should say “Insufficient Evidence” rather than make a negative inference.
01

Job Intelligence

Convert natural-language job descriptions into structured role profiles, responsibilities, must-have skills and preferred qualifications.

02

Competency Mapping

Translate requirements into knowledge, skills, abilities and experience, with explicit competency dimensions and weights.

03

Evidence-based Screening

Every assessment should be traceable to evidence found in the candidate's resume. Unsupported claims are not treated as facts.

04

Structured Interview

Generate STAR behavioral questions that target specific competencies and areas where evidence remains limited.

05

Human-in-the-loop

HireLens AI provides structured reference information. It does not make hiring, rejection or ranking decisions.

06

Bias Mitigation

The assessment framework is designed to avoid relying on irrelevant personal attributes such as name, gender or age.
