Project Methodology

Evidence before judgment.

HireLens AI is designed around a simple principle: AI should organize evidence, reveal uncertainty and support structured evaluation — not make the hiring decision.

01

Job Intelligence

Analyze the job description to identify responsibilities, requirements and the capabilities the role actually demands.

02

Competency Mapping

Translate job requirements into observable competency dimensions that can later be assessed consistently.

03

Evidence-based Screening

Assess candidates through explicit evidence in the resume rather than assumptions based on personal characteristics.

04

Structured Interview

Use targeted STAR-style questions to investigate important competencies and areas where evidence is incomplete.

05

Human-in-the-loop

AI produces an analytical reference. HR professionals retain responsibility for interpretation and final decisions.

06

Bias Mitigation

Ignore unnecessary personal information and avoid negative conclusions when the resume simply does not provide enough evidence.

Core Principle — “No evidence, no claim.” If the resume does not provide evidence for a competency, HireLens AI should return “Insufficient Evidence” rather than inventing or inferring a negative conclusion.
