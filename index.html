Responsible AI Principles
Designed as decision support — not an automated hiring authority.
Evidence First Do not make claims unsupported by the candidate's materials.
Insufficient Evidence Missing information is treated as an evidence gap, not a negative assumption.
Fairness Do not evaluate protected characteristics such as gender, age, or ethnicity.
Human Oversight Final hiring decisions remain with human reviewers.
